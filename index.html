Why this one

There is no server. That's the feature.

Most meeting recorders upload your audio to a third-party server. KinetBrief doesn't — not as a policy, but structurally. Transcription, the AI summary, and the mind map are all computed on your iPhone's chip. There is nowhere for your audio to go.

✓On-device transcription (Apple Speech + WhisperKit)
✓On-device AI summary + mind map
✓No servers, no accounts, no telemetry
✓Recordings encrypted at rest on your device
✓iCloud sync is your private CloudKit database — Apple holds it, we can't read it
✓Zero outbound network requests while you record or transcribe
HIPAA-sensitive & privilege-sensitive by design. Because audio never leaves the device, KinetBrief fits workflows where cloud recorders can't go. We don't claim "compliance" — we claim something stronger: nothing is transmitted.
